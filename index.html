Guide

Why Clean Links?

Copied URLs often contain campaign tags, ad click IDs, social tracking values, and email marketing identifiers. Those parameters can make links long, ugly, and more revealing than needed. LinkClean Shelf keeps useful destination parameters while removing common tracking clutter.

Cleaner Sharing

Shorter links are easier to read in emails, chats, documents, and posts.

Privacy Hygiene

Remove common click IDs before forwarding links to friends, clients, or public pages.

Browser-Only

The first version runs locally in your browser without sending pasted links away.
